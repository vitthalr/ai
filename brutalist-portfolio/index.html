ABOUT ME

I'm a digital designer and developer with a passion for creating raw, unfiltered digital experiences that stand out in a world of templated designs.

My work focuses on brutalist principles: exposing the structural elements of design, embracing asymmetry, and creating bold, functional interfaces that refuse to hide behind unnecessary ornamentation.

Based in New York City, I've collaborated with brands who aren't afraid to make a statement and challenge conventional aesthetics.
